Recruiter activity

All branches

Starts booked today

Placements entered in BOLD today, by placement branch, client and the recruiter credited with the hire. "Start date" is the day the person is due to begin work.

Calls and recruiting work by person

Each bar is one person's manual activity in BOLD. "Connected, interview booked" and "Interview logged" are often two entries for the same booking, and "Interview logged" also includes confirmation calls on interviews booked earlier. The "interviews booked" total at the top counts each person once.

Sense texts by person

Texts sent under each person's name, and candidate replies back

Sense posts both one-off texts and bulk sends under the recruiter's name, so large spikes are campaigns, not hand-typed texts.

Hour by hour

Times are when the entry was saved in BOLD (Eastern). A late-day pile-up usually means activity was entered in a batch rather than as calls happened.

Onboards and interview show rate

Show rate = of the candidates a recruiter logged "Interview Scheduled" for in the last 7 days, how many have since been marked "Onboarding Complete". Recent bookings haven't happened yet, so the rate climbs through the week. People with fewer than 10 interviews are greyed out.

Source: Avionté DataLink (Snowflake), APPACTIVITY joined to users and offices, . DataLink runs about 30–60 minutes behind BOLD.

How these numbers are built
  1. Workforce, hours, overtime and bill rate: Commercial Employees billing to OWI New Kensington, Jan 1 – Oct 13, 2026. Blended rate $22.80 straight time, 1.6% of hours overtime at 1.5×.
  2. Lease to own, per robot hour for a Simple / Standard / Complex station: Spark $18.00 / $19.75 / $23.50, Core $20.00 / $21.75 / $25.50, Thor $22.00 / $23.75 / $27.50, with a tooling and infeed allowance set per station at the site survey built in (Simple $10,000; Standard $20,000; Complex $40,000). Billed on 2,080 hours a year for 36 months, then a $1 buyout of the robot and its tooling; service and insurance included during the term. Anything beyond a station's allowance is a change order you approve first. Robot temps: $1/hr below the lease rate, less another $0.75 at 5–9 robots and $1.50 at 10 or more, 36-month term; Madler Robotics keeps ownership. Billing starts after each station's acceptance test.
  3. A robot runs every scheduled hour of its station: 8-hour shifts, 5 days, 50 weeks, one or two shifts as selected. Oversight is 1 hour of a lead's time per robot per shift at the temp rate. After month 36 an owned robot is shown with a support plan at 10% of list a year. The support plan is priced off Standard Bots published starting prices (Spark $29,500, Core $39,500, Thor $49,500, Oct 2026). Final pricing follows a site survey.
  4. Turnover cost: your inputs above. Injury figures: OSHA (share of injuries in a worker's first year) and National Safety Council Injury Facts (average workers' comp claim, 2022–23 claims).

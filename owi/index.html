How these numbers are built
  1. Workforce, hours, overtime and bill rate: Commercial Employees billing to OWI New Kensington, Jan 1 – Oct 13, 2026. Blended rate $22.80 straight time, 1.6% of hours overtime at 1.5×.
  2. Robot temp rates ($15/hr packing and line loading, $16 palletizing, $13 pick and place) include the robot, tooling, install, support, software, insurance and a swap if it goes down. Oversight is 1 hour per robot per shift at the temp rate. Two 8-hour shifts, 5 days, 50 weeks.
  3. Lease to own: robot plus install at 5% over 36 months, $1 buyout, plus a 10% yearly support plan. Purchase: Standard Bots published starting prices (Spark $29,500, Core $39,500, Thor $49,500, Oct 2026) plus install estimates, spread over 5 years. Final pricing follows a site survey.
  4. Turnover cost: your inputs above. Injury figures: OSHA (share of injuries in a worker's first year) and National Safety Council Injury Facts (average workers' comp claim, 2022–23 claims).

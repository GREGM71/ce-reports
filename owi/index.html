What a robot temp is

Not a six-month automation project. A worker you can move.

Traditional automation is bolted to one line, behind a cage, built by engineers for one product. Robot temps are collaborative arms on a rolling base that your own people run, and that move to wherever the work is this week.

Own it or rent it

No capital request

Lease to own by the hour for 36 months and the robot and its tooling are yours for $1. Or keep it as a robot temp, $1 an hour less, that we own, insure and maintain. Either way it's on the same invoice as your staffing.

Phase 1 · how we start

Four stations, one 36-month commitment.

Payments don't start until each robot proves itself on your line.

Walk the line

Together we pick four stations with the most turnover: case packing, palletizing, line loading and pick and place.

Survey and set up

We engineer how product reaches each robot, then install and program the cell with Standard Bots. Each station is rated Simple, Standard or Complex, and its tooling allowance goes into the hourly rate.

Acceptance test

Each station has to hit the rate we agree on before any billing starts. We train your leads on the floor while it runs.

36 months, then it's yours

The station's rate on 2,080 hours a year. At month 36 you buy the robot and its tooling for $1. We keep supporting it, building routines and covering it with people if it's ever down.

How these numbers are built
  1. Workforce, hours, overtime and bill rate: Commercial Employees billing to OWI New Kensington, Jan 1 – Oct 13, 2026. Blended rate $22.80 straight time, 1.6% of hours overtime at 1.5×.
  2. Lease to own: Spark $20, Core $22, Thor $24 per robot hour, plus a tooling and infeed allowance set per station at the site survey (Simple $10,000, +$2.00/hr; Standard $20,000, +$3.75/hr; Complex $40,000, +$7.50/hr). Billed on 2,080 hours a year for 36 months, then a $1 buyout of the robot and its tooling; service and insurance included during the term. Anything beyond a station's allowance is a change order you approve first. Robot temps: $1/hr below the lease rate, less another $0.75 at 5–9 robots and $1.50 at 10 or more, 36-month term; Madler Robotics keeps ownership. Billing starts after each station's acceptance test.
  3. A robot runs every scheduled hour of its station: 8-hour shifts, 5 days, 50 weeks, one or two shifts as selected. Oversight is 1 hour of a lead's time per robot per shift at the temp rate. After month 36 an owned robot is shown with a support plan at 10% of list a year. Purchase: Standard Bots published starting prices (Spark $29,500, Core $39,500, Thor $49,500, Oct 2026) plus the same tooling and infeed allowance. Final pricing follows a site survey.
  4. Turnover cost: your inputs above. Injury figures: OSHA (share of injuries in a worker's first year) and National Safety Council Injury Facts (average workers' comp claim, 2022–23 claims).
